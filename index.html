Claims Card

Weekly jobless-claims threshold prices, three models, live.

How this works

Three models, one formula. Each model is a normal distribution over next week's claims print:

  • No-change (blue): center = last print, spread 13.0K.
  • Consensus blend (orange): center = last print moved 50% toward the analyst consensus, spread 12.4K.
  • Forecast blend (green): center = last print moved 90% toward the TE model forecast, spread 12.0K.

P(over T) = Phi((center − T) / spread), where Phi is the standard normal CDF. The spreads come from each model's measured correlation with the weekly change on 58 test weeks of leakage-safe data: variance shrinks by rho², so the challengers are tighter.

The spread constants are provisional. The two challenger models carry real but unproven edges (+0.02 and +0.00 log score vs no-change, within noise). The 84-week prospective test running now settles it. Treat the card as a pricing baseline, not a crystal ball.

Known blind spot: in every big-move week in the test sample (13 of 13), all three models missed in the same direction. Averaging correlated opinions cannot see a move nobody saw. If a market prices a big move, that is information this card does not contain.

For research. Not advice. No change is the champion until proven otherwise.
